How it works

Getting started is simple.

No complicated intake forms. No waitlists. Just a real conversation to see if we're a good fit.

01

Reach out

Call, text, or fill out the contact form. Tell me a little about what brings you here no details are too small or too big. I'll respond within 24 hours.

02

Free consultation

We'll have a relaxed 15-minute conversation over the phone. You can ask anything you'd like, and together we'll decide if it's the right fit. Zero pressure.

03

Begin your sessions

We meet weekly via video from wherever you feel most comfortable. Your couch, your office, your car. You set the pace, and I'll be right there with you.

Services Offered

Comprehensive therapy tailored to your unique needs.

Virtual sessions for Individual, Couples, and Family Therapy, plus in-person presentations and corporate trainings.

Individual Therapy

One-on-one sessions for anyone looking for a supportive environment to explore feelings and behaviors that get in the way of your happiness.

Couples Therapy

Sessions for you and your partner to resolve struggles that might be getting in the way of your connection.

Family Therapy

For parent-child, siblings, co-parents, or any group wanting to work on their relationship dynamic together.

Group Therapy

A space for people to come together and explore a common struggle or situation with mutual support and professional guidance.

Presentations & Consulting

Virtual and in-person presentations on Self-Care, Mindfulness, Diversity & Inclusion, and more. Custom topics available upon request.
